# Nexora — 100% gross credited principal, gross reward basis, independent company payouts

**Status: DRAFT / NOT DEPLOYED / NOT AUDITED. No production deposits or financial transfers have been enabled.**

## Final clarification from platform owner

The depositor must see **100% of their deposit** in the account and any conditional reward computation must use that **same 100% gross amount**, even though the owner requests 6% and ten company wallets 1.4% each to be associated with each deposit and reinvest.

**A visible user principal of 1,000 USDT must be a REAL, fully backed principal liability of 1,000 USDT.** It must not be a fictional display of a balance backed by only 800 USDT. This is a non-negotiable accounting and investor-disclosure safeguard.

### Correct funded interpretation — 1,000 USDT new deposit

| Item | Amount | Source / destination |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| User transfers | 1,000 USDT | 100% held in user-principal vault |
| Account displays user principal | **1,000 USDT** | Full user liability in vault |
| Reward calculation basis | **1,000 USDT** | Conditional tier cap, not guaranteed yield |
| Owner receives | 60 USDT | **Extra company funding**, NOT user's deposit |
| Company wallet 1 through 10 each receives | 14 USDT | **Extra company funding**, NOT user's deposit |
| Company bonus budget required | **200 USDT** | Independently funded before company payout |
| Combined resources needed | **1,200 USDT** | 1,000 user deposit + 200 additional corporate funding |
| Retained in principal vault | **1,000 USDT** | User principal is fully backed |

If the company does not have **an additional 200 USDT** from a legitimate source, it cannot pay these company bonuses **and** truthfully credit 1,000 USDT as the user's protected principal. Merely reporting the old gross balance would conceal a 20% shortfall. The system must either fund those external payouts independently, or not distribute them.

### Correct funded interpretation — 100 USDT Reinvest

- Only a **previously funded and credited reward liability** may be reinvested.
- Convert **all 100 USDT** from credited reward liability into a new 90-day principal tranche. User principal rises by **100 USDT**; **no part of it is transferred to owner or partners**.
- Conditional reward tiers see the full 100 USDT increase after qualification.
- The company must supply **another 20 USDT of outside funds** to grant 6 USDT to owner and 1.4 USDT to each of ten company wallets.
- Vault token balance and combined user liabilities remain unchanged during liability conversion; only the extra company funding supports the separate bonuses.
- Reinvest does NOT increase externally deposited basis used for the cumulative 20% per 24-hour withdrawal limit in this draft.

### Withdrawal and reward rules

- **Withdrawal fee: 0%.**
- **Principal maturity: 90 days**, tracked individually per deposit/reinvest tranche.
- **Daily withdrawal ceiling:** maximum gross withdrawals and reward claims in an anchored 24-hour window is 20% of the user's *externally deposited basis*; no double-spending by splitting across transactions.
- With 1,000 USDT of eligible externally deposited basis, that ceiling is **200 USDT per window**, if enough unlocked, backed balance exists. Reinvested credits do not silently increase the withdrawal-cap denominator.
- **Profit is not generated by making deposits or reinvesting.** Daily rates of 1–2.5% are conditional maximums, *never guaranteed*, and depend on independently funded, realized profits, operational eligibility and sufficient rewards reserves. Payouts may be zero.

### Implementation boundaries

The **current unaudited source branch** already keeps 100% of each deposit in the user's vault and computes conditional reward ceilings from recorded principal. The Solidity `NexoraEntryAllocationPreview.sol` and its tests now model the **independent extra company funding needed** for the owner and ten companies. This preview is **pure math**: it never accepts a deposit, never pays bonuses and cannot debit capital.

The existing `NexoraRevenueRouter.sol` and `NexoraRevenueSplitter.sol` are built for independent business-revenue funding; they are **NOT automatically connected to every new user deposit or every reinvest**. They must not be advertised as automated 6%/1.4% deposit bonuses. Implementing a production deposit-linked commission path would require transaction-linked proof, duplicate prevention, a fully prefunded independent company pool, operational caps, corporate governance, consent and independent audit. **The vault must never pay company bonuses out of principal.**

A user interface may show *Deposited 1,000 USDT*, *Protected principal 1,000 USDT* and *Reward calculation basis 1,000 USDT* **only when on-chain vault accounts and token balances actually back these claims**. It must not display unsupported balances or guaranteed yield.
